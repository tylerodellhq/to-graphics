# TO Graphics changes

## 1.0.6
- Previews are drawn at each template's real shape: 9:16 ones are tall and thin like the comp, with no black box around them.

## 1.0.5
- Simpler client window: just the name. Auto-sorting is one plain line with a Change link, and it's learnt when you move a template into a client.

## 1.0.4
- Rename clients: Settings > Clients, the Rename chip next to the chosen client, or click the chosen client again. (Double-click never worked: the chips redraw on the first click.)

## 1.0.3
- Rename templates from More (or double-click the name): changes the name Premiere shows and the file name; client, type and preview stay.

## 1.0.2
- Previews wait for the template to load: a red Media Offline frame is spotted and rendered again, and the busiest real frame is kept (previews are now PNGs). Old previews are remade once.
- Client headings show names exactly as typed (no forced capitals).

## 1.0.1
- Star on each preview for favourites, Small/Medium/Large preview size, Show in Finder, Open folder, and Remove from my templates (a copy is kept in .to-graphics/removed).

## 1.0.0
- First version: your templates sorted by client (auto from names like "HL …"), filters by type and shape,
  real previews rendered by Premiere at each template's own shape, Captioneer and hidden templates kept out
  of the way, add at the playhead, Add button for .mogrt files, new versions swapped into the open project
  with text and settings kept, updates through Creative Cloud.
